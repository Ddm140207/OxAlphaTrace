**OxAlphaTrace** is a behavioral fingerprinting study of the model "Ox Alpha" (per `master.md`). Its purpose is to determine whether *observable behavior* ΓÇö identity claims, reasoning patterns, style, refusals, benchmarks, multilingual/coding performance ΓÇö can serve as a measurable fingerprint for model comparison, attribution, and provenance analysis, without assuming self-reported identity is truthful or claiming to recover hidden architecture/training lineage. Conclusions are framed as evidence-based hypotheses about behavioral similarity to reference model families, not definitive provenance claims.
