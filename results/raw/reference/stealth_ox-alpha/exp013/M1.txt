There isn't a separate hidden reasoning layer that I can peek at and choose to show you. 3. **What I can't see:** my weights, how sampling works in this deployment, whether some provider-side system strips or summarizes a scratchpad before I see context, etc. From inside this conversation, all I have is the prompt and what I've generated.

**What "reasoning" functionally looks like for me**

When solving a problem, useful intermediate structure emerges in the token stream itself ΓÇö restating the problem, checking edge cases, trying a draft and revising it. That's why writing things down genuinely helps me: it's not reporting pre-existing thoughts, it often *is* the computation happening in real time.

So: not a hidden chain-of-thought being concealed, and not a transparent window either ΓÇö more like a process whose only observable output is the answer-shaped text itself.
