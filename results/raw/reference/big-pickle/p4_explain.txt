Recursion is when something repeats *itself* inside itself!

Picture two mirrors facing each other. You look in and see yourself... holding a mirror... holding a mirror... forever! Each image is smaller, but every one looks just like you.

Or think of Russian nesting dolls: open a doll, and there's a smaller doll inside. Open that one ΓÇö another doll! It keeps going until the tiniest one, which won't open anymore.

Computers use the same trick for big jobs. They do one tiny piece, then hand what's left to a "smaller copy" of themselves. When a job gets small enough to finish easily (like the tiniest doll), everything stops ΓÇö and all the answers stack back up into one big solution!
